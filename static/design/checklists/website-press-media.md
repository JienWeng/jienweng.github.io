# Press / Media — Website

A page providing journalists, analysts, and content creators with the resources they need to cover the company accurately.

Source: https://www.checklist.design/website/press-media

## Items

### Company overview
A concise, factual description of the company, what it does, when it was founded, and who it serves

_Tip: A press overview written as a ready-to-use paragraph saves journalists time. The easier it is to lift and paste, the more often it gets used_

### Press coverage
A curated list of notable media mentions with publication name, headline, and link

_Tip: Logos of well-known publications displayed visually signal credibility faster than a text list_

### Press contact
A dedicated email address for media enquiries so journalists know exactly where to reach the right person or team

### Brand assets
Downloadable logos, product screenshots, and founder headshots in multiple formats

_Tip: Light and dark logo versions are both routinely needed by journalists, since a press kit without both creates unnecessary back-and-forth_

### Key statistics
Notable numbers that journalists can use to characterise the company e.g. customers, revenue milestones, countries, team size 

### Recent press releases
Archived official announcements for journalists researching the company's history
