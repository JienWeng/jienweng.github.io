# Event Page — Website

A page promoting a specific event — a conference, webinar, workshop, or meetup — and driving registrations.

Source: https://www.checklist.design/website/event-page

## Items

### Event name and date
The name of the event and when it is happening, shown prominently at the top of the page

_Tip: Include the timezone explicitly for online events_

### Event description
What the event is, who it is for, and what attendees will get from it

### Speakers, presenters or hosts
Names, photos, and brief bios of the people presenting or facilitating

_Tip: Speaker credibility is often the deciding factor for whether someone registers. It tends to be the most-read section of an event page_

### Agenda or schedule
A session-by-session or time-slot breakdown of what will happen during the event

### Registration CTA
A clear form or button to register, with pricing if applicable

_Tip: Flagging ticket availability close to CTA helps communicate scarcity_

### Location or platform details
For in-person events, the venue address and travel information. For online events, the platform being used
