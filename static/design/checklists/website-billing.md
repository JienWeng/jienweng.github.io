# Billing — Website

A screen where users manage information regarding payment, subscription and billing.

Source: https://www.checklist.design/website/billing

## Items

### Payment method/s used
Display payment method details with ability to update or remove. A subscription service may want to prevent all methods being removed as it will become an issue later.

### History
A list of past charges with the ability to download invoices

_Tip: Downloadable invoice format should be PDF to be easily shared in other reporting tools_

### Plan details
Details of current plan includes price, features and next billing date

_Tip: Have a clear action to upgrade or change plan in this area_

### Promo or discount code
A field to apply a code to activate a discount or deal

### Additional details
Information that influences billing or plan decision making e.g. seats, roles, usage, metrics

### Next payment
If subscription, show details of upcoming payment including amount, date and method

### Cancel subscription
A clear action to cancel and stop payments
