# Privacy — Website

Page covering the legal terms of using the product (privacy policy, terms of service, and cookie policy) written clearly and kept current.

Source: https://www.checklist.design/website/legal-privacy

## Items

### Privacy policy
A clear document explaining what data is collected, how it is used, how long it is kept, and how users can request deletion

_Tip: A plain-language summary at the top is an option, to allow most users understand the policy without diving into deeper details unless necessary_

### Terms of service
The agreement governing use of the product (user responsibilities, acceptable use, limitation of liability)

### Cookie policy
A clear explanation of which cookies are used, what they do, and how users can control them

_Tip: Cookie policies separated from the privacy policy are increasingly what users search for, since they serve different functions and are regulated differently in most jurisdictions_

### Last updated date
A clear timestamp on every legal document so users know if they are reading the current version

### Version history or changelog
For products with engaged users, a log of what changed between policy versions — builds trust and reduces enquiries.

_Tip: A summary of changes in plain English at the top of an updated policy is far more reassuring than just changing the date._

### Contact for legal queries
A dedicated email or address for data, privacy, or legal enquiries as required by most regulations.
