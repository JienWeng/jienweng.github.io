I’m Jien Weng, an independent consultant and product manager. I coordinate delivery for CryptoRails and work with Quandatics on the internal CRM linking sales, project delivery, and finance. I also teach data science and machine learning at Quandatics Academy.

At Monash University Malaysia, I research wastewater treatment plant prediction using RNNs, Transformers, and Markov chain methods. This work includes the Monash Water Platform, with the CitizenFlood Android app and operator dashboard. Explore the wastewater monitoring project. My other research interests include multi-agent reinforcement learning, market microstructure, and quantitative finance.

I studied Applied Mathematics with Computing at UTAR. My notes contain the derivations and worked examples I use to understand statistics, optimisation, and reinforcement learning.
