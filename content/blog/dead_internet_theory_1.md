LinkedIn and Reddit feel different lately. I keep seeing polished posts that say the right things but feel detached from any person. It may be a real shift, or my own overreaction.

{{ figure(src="https://cdn.cosmos.so/b77a432b-7713-4b10-879d-e8256d284766?format=jpeg", alt="You can't tell whether the experience is real or not") }}

I have used ChatGPT to help write before. Reading it back, it didn’t sound like anything I had lived. The sentences were clean; the experience was artificial.

Chatbots are good for proofreading, restructuring, and catching the sentence I somehow wrote twice. They should not supply the experience. I would rather read a long-ass, badly organised article by someone who was there than a perfect post assembled from general opinions.

I can’t tell if I’m seeing more bots, more generated content, or just noticing both more. If you’ve noticed it too, [email me](mailto:laijienweng@gmail.com).
