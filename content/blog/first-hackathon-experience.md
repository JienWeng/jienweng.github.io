Janice, Roius, Gwyn, and I entered PayHack 2024 as four UTAR mathematics students. Two of us had done a hackathon, Gwyn had never coded, and none of us knew much web development.

We made the Top 32 of almost 100 teams, then had four days, around our internships, to learn enough Vue.js and Flask for a prototype. Janice travelled from JB to KL for it.

![Team breakfast at PayHack 2024](https://cdn.cosmos.so/25b6ad42-8235-45ae-9505-a2c296a8ca2a?format=jpeg)

## The idea and the questions we hadn’t answered

Our idea, MouManTai, estimated carbon footprints from transaction data and explored pooling the savings for trading.

Johan Nasir’s mentoring felt like a rotan session. “What happens if the carbon credits are manipulated?” “How do you ensure the footprint data is authentic?” Each one sent us back to our assumptions.

![Team discussing the prototype with a mentor](https://cdn.cosmos.so/b16e0c66-cefb-4cc6-8bf6-de5a087cc513?format=jpeg)

## Keeping the demo alive

We did tutorials after our internships. At the hackathon we slept in turns; I couldn’t get a tent, so I took four-hour naps on a bench.

![Working on the prototype overnight](https://cdn.cosmos.so/0d444922-77c4-4d91-b645-fb96b7fd5d17?format=jpeg)

At 8:30 AM on submission day, 90 minutes before the deadline, our backend crashed while parsing data. We hardcoded some components and submitted by 10 AM.

![Presenting MouManTai, our carbon-footprint tracking and trading prototype](https://cdn.cosmos.so/0784bdc4-dc31-4b2b-9d5a-2e04677a9ba1?format=jpeg)

## The pitch

We didn’t make the Top 10. A blockchain remittance team stood out: they had researched regulation and validated their market as carefully as they built the product.

The stronger pitches tied features to business problems, survived demo pressure, and answered questions with numbers. Next time, I wanted to validate the market earlier and practise the pitch more.

I did win a Samsung monitor in the lucky draw, which gave our tired team a laugh.

![Winning a Samsung monitor in the lucky draw](https://cdn.cosmos.so/3d119ab7-d3bf-4632-8675-6fbaf1963c08?format=jpeg)

![Jien Weng, Janice, Gwyn and Roius](https://cdn.cosmos.so/266c9030-58ad-4536-9e60-c88edb6df8a9?format=jpeg)

Janice, Roius, and Gwyn took the risk with me. Johan asked the questions we wanted to avoid. PayNet and JomHack gave us a place to test an idea outside the classroom.
