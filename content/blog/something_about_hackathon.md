Hackathons demand an insane amount of time and energy. Preliminaries, semi-finals, and finals each mean more brainstorming, last-minute changes, and almost no sleep.

At first, I finished each one looking forward to the next. After enough rounds, I was tired before the next one started.

I also kept comparing myself to people who work in tech every day. I’m a mathematics student exploring outside my field, which is easy to forget when everyone seems more experienced.

One more hackathon is coming up. I’ll give it what I can, then take a proper break. I still want to do hackathons, but I need time away from constantly proving myself.
