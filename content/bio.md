I’m Jien Weng, an independent consultant and product manager. At CryptoRails, I maintain the product backlog, define acceptance criteria, and coordinate engineering delivery for multi-chain crypto payments and wallet infrastructure.

At Quandatics, I work on business transformation through an [internal CRM](@/blog/quandatics-crm-business-transformation.md) linking sales, project delivery, and finance. My role spans requirements, design, development, deployment, and support. Separately, I teach and coach learners in data science and machine learning at Quandatics Academy.

Earlier, as a Finance Analyst - Data Engineer Intern at Great Eastern Life Assurance, I worked on data pipelines using Hive SQL in Apache Zeppelin.

At Monash University Malaysia, I research wastewater treatment plant prediction using RNNs, Transformers, and Markov chain methods. This includes the [Monash Water Platform](@/projects/monash-water.md), CitizenFlood Android app, and operator dashboard. My other research interests include multi-agent reinforcement learning, market microstructure, and quantitative finance.

I also build [VideoFlow](@/projects/videoflow.md), a canvas-based AI video studio for web and desktop, and [PolyArb](@/projects/polyarb.md), a Rust Polymarket arbitrage platform now in production.

I studied Applied Mathematics with Computing at UTAR. I write [notes](@/notes/_index.md) on statistics, optimisation, and reinforcement learning.

For my experience and qualifications, see my [CV](@/cv/_index.md). To discuss a role or project, [email me](mailto:laijienweng@gmail.com).
