# Compare — Website

A page that positions the product directly against a specific competitor, helping prospects who are evaluating alternatives to make a decision.

Source: https://www.checklist.design/website/compare-page

## Items

### Clear positioning statement
An opening that states plainly how this product differs from the competitor being compared

_Tip: A compare page that claims superiority on every dimension reads as untrustworthy so include honest acknowledgement of trade-offs for more credibility_

### Feature comparison table
A side-by-side table of key features showing what each product includes, with clear tick and cross indicators

_Tip: This table focused on decision-relevant features 

This table doesn't have to mention every single feature, just the most persuasive and relevant to influencing decision_

### Key differentiators
A section expanding on the areas where this product genuinely excels beyond a table comparison

### Social proof
Testimonials or quotes from customers who switched from the competitor being compared

### Pricing comparison
A clear, fair comparison of pricing structures if the difference is a meaningful factor for prospects

_Tip: Pricing comparisons only work best if there's a genuine advantage e.g. cheaper or more value for money spent_

### CTA
A direct prompt to try the product or book a demo
