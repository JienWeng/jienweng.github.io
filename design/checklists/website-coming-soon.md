# Coming Soon — Website

A placeholder page shown before a product or feature launches, designed to capture interest and build an early audience.

Source: https://www.checklist.design/website/coming-soon

## Items

### What is coming
A clear description of what is being built, enough to generate genuine interest without overpromising

_Tip: The most effective coming soon pages give a product direction, specific enough to be compelling, vague enough not to overpromise_

### Email capture
A simple signup form to collect email addresses from interested visitors before launch

_Tip: A tangible incentive for signing up (early access, a discount, or exclusive updates) consistently outperforms a bare email field in conversion_

### Launch date or timeline
An indication of when the product will be available, even if it is approximate

_Tip: When the exact date is uncertain, a quarter or season is still more useful than no timeline at all, since even approximate dates create anticipation_

### Social links
Links to social profiles where visitors can follow progress and updates

### Brand presence
A page that is visually on brand even before the product exists
